Regions

Markets we serve

BST concentrates wholesale support on three priority regions. Tell us your destination country and channel — we will align categories, packing and quotation details accordingly.

Region 01

Africa

Across African trade corridors, phone accessories remain a high-frequency retail category. Distributors need dependable chargers, cables and audio products that can be restocked without constant SKU churn.

BST works with importers and wholesale partners who supply city and regional retailers. We discuss plug preferences, carton mixes and MOQs that fit both first shipments and ongoing replenishment — including mixed loads when a full single-SKU container is not yet practical.

Typical conversations cover entry-to-mid retail price points, durable packaging for inland transit, and clear labelling for warehouse handling.

Region 02

Middle East

The Middle East hosts established wholesale hubs that redistribute across local and neighbouring markets. Buyers often look for polished presentation, consistent quality and assortments that work for both mainstream retail and gift-oriented channels.

BST supports importers serving GCC and nearby wholesale networks with headphones, earphones, charging products and complementary accessories. Quotes can address retail-ready packing alongside bulk options for further redistribution.

We welcome briefings on target cities, preferred connectors and any packaging language or labelling requirements you need for local compliance and shelf standards.

Wholesale hubs Retail-ready packs GCC & nearby Gift & travel sets

Region 03

Central Asia

Central Asian markets continue to expand mobile accessory demand as smartphone ownership grows. Partners often need a balanced starter assortment — charging essentials plus audio — that can scale as retail coverage widens.

BST engages with wholesalers and importers who are building or refreshing accessory lines. We emphasise practical MOQs, durable transit packaging and product mixes that avoid overstocking slow variants.

Share your destination markets and channel type (wholesale cash-and-carry, retail chains or mixed distribution). We will respond with category suggestions and a wholesale quotation path.
