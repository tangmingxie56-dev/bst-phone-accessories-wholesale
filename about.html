About BST

BST Phone Accessories Wholesale is a B2B supplier of mobile accessories for distributors and importers who need reliable bulk supply into growing markets.

Our approach

Wholesale partners first

BST exists to simplify accessory sourcing for businesses that sell onward — wholesalers, importers and retail chains. We concentrate on categories that move consistently: headphones and earphones, chargers, charging cables, plus complementary lines such as cases, power banks and adapters.

Our focus markets are Africa, the Middle East and Central Asia. That means we pay attention to practical details that matter in those corridors — packaging durability, clear product descriptions, flexible order sizes for new partners, and communication that supports export paperwork and shipping timelines.

We do not overstate what we cannot verify. Quotes, samples and shipment plans are handled case by case so both sides understand scope, lead times and commercial terms before goods move.

Whether you are testing a new assortment or replenishing established SKUs, we aim for straightforward collaboration: honest availability, competitive wholesale pricing and follow-through after the order is confirmed.
